PLATE 03 · THE INTERLOCK

01

BRIDGE

Runtimes don't talk. OpenClaw gateways speak HTTP chat, Hermes speaks ssh. fleet-bridge makes them one roster behind one tool surface — cross-runtime by construction.

02

SUPERVISE

Supervisors supervise. wranglebot makes thrashing expensive: dangerous constructs denied before execution, runaway loops killed on deterministic counts, everything recorded.

03

AGREE

Frameworks coordinate. Tractum agrees: per-agent signed stances, quorum-verifiable receipts you can hand to an auditor, a regulator, or a future teammate.
